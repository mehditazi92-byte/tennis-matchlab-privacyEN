Privacy Policy

Tennis MatchLab

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Tennis MatchLab ("the App") handles information when you use the application. We respect your privacy and aim to be transparent about how information is processed.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Tennis MatchLab allows you to record tennis scores and review match statistics. Information you enter, such as match scores and statistics, may be stored locally on your device to provide the App's features.

The App does not require you to create an account to use its core features. Information stored locally may be lost if you uninstall the App or clear its application data.

3. Personal Information

We do not ask you to provide your name, postal address, or account details to use the App's core features. Please do not include sensitive personal information in any information you choose to enter.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The App may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising services, such as Google AdMob, if advertising features are enabled.

These providers may collect or process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, and advertising interaction data, subject to their own policies and applicable settings. Such information may be used to deliver, measure, or personalize advertisements, where permitted.

For more information about Google's data practices, please visit: Google Privacy Policy.

5. Data Sharing

Match information stored locally is not routinely sent to us by the App. Third-party services integrated into the App may process information as described in this policy. We do not sell personal information.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Match information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App's stored data. Data handled by third-party providers is subject to their own retention and deletion policies.

7. Children's Privacy

Tennis MatchLab is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe that personal information has been collected inappropriately, please contact us.

8. Your Privacy Choices

You can manage relevant device privacy and advertising settings through your device settings. Where required, consent choices for advertising and tracking will be presented in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
